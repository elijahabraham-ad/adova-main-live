The mark.

The O is a tennis ball, the seam is a smile, and the squiggle carries the whole name. Built from the three concepts she liked, drawn as vector, and outlined so the file needs no font installed anywhere.

88 / 88 / 32 / 16 px
Two ball versions on purpose. The wordmark uses the face, because the word around it already says tennis. The standalone icon keeps the side seams, because on its own it has to read as a tennis ball first and a smile second.

Where it came from

The four concepts that led here are still up: Smile Seam, The O, Court Monogram, Bounce. She picked pieces of three of them and this is the combination.

Type is Archivo, outlined to paths, so nothing depends on a font being installed. Colors are navy #12263F and ball yellow #DFF34B, and they can move if a brand direction calls for it. Pick a site direction next.
